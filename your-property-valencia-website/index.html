Your strategic partner, not a salesperson

My name is Sara

I'm originally from a town near Barcelona and I've lived in sunny Valencia for several years now. I got into real estate because I wanted to learn and build my own portfolio, to have a bit more freedom — and at 30, with just three properties, I had the financial security to choose the path I wanted to follow.

After many years at a large real estate agency, I felt increasingly drawn to working independently — guiding investors in a more personal, honest and strategic way.

Today I work as an independent intermediary for investors looking for the right opportunities in Spain. As an investor myself, I look beyond just a beautiful property — I look for the hidden potential of distressed assets. My focus is always the long term: what strategy is best for your long-term goals? Does the location fit your strategy? Is it future-proof? What about return, regulation and growth potential?

Most important, I don't sell houses — I provide a comprehensive, worry-free service, always protecting your interest. From viewings and negotiations to contracts, municipal registrations, legal coordination and the final transfer, I take care of the whole process. Transparent, dedicated and personal.

My goal? That you invest with confidence, clarity and peace of mind. — Sara Muñoz, founder of Your Property Valencia
