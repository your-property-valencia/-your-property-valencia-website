Overview

Located in Benimaclet, one of Valencia's most dynamic and sought-after neighborhoods, this property benefits from the area's mix of local charm, cultural life, and proximity to universities and public transport. The strong rental demand from students and young professionals makes Benimaclet a prime location for long-term investment.

Acquisition & Renovation

Acquired directly from a bank, the apartment was originally a three-bedroom unit with a small, outdated kitchen. A full renovation was carried out, including a new open-plan kitchen, updated bathroom, complete electrical upgrade, and fresh paint throughout. The layout was optimized by adding an additional room within the living area, enhancing both functionality and rental potential.

Key Improvements

  • Acquired from bank at below-market price
  • Complete kitchen renovation with modern open-plan design
  • Full electrical system upgrade
  • Additional bedroom created from living space
  • Enhanced rental appeal in high-demand area

Overview

Located in Torrefiel, near one of the area's main parks, this property sits in an emerging neighborhood with growing demand among students and young families. The area offers good transport links and improving amenities, making it a strong candidate for value growth.

Acquisition & Renovation

Acquired recently, this apartment was a hidden gem, overlooked due to its poor presentation, outdated furniture, and smell of smoke.
