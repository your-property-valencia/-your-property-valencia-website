Cookie policy

Last updated: 02 November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies are small files or entries that a website stores on your device. They can be used to keep a service working, to remember your choices, or to measure and advertise.

What this website uses

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. This website does not measure your visit or track you across other sites.
  • One preference entry. If you close the newsletter pop-up, your browser stores a small entry (local storage, name ypv-newsletter-dismissed) so the pop-up does not appear again for 30 days. It holds only the date and is never sent to a server. It is a user-preference item that does not need consent.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. The typeface is loaded from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • Links to other sites. Calendly (booking), MailerLite (newsletter) and Google (reviews) open on their own websites, which have their own cookie policies.

How to manage cookies

You can delete or block cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. Blocking the preference entry above only means the newsletter pop-up may appear again.

Changes

If this website starts using analytics, advertising or other cookies that need consent, this policy will be updated and a consent banner will be added first.
