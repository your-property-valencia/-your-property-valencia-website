2. What data I collect, why, and on what basis

  • Contact form. Name, email, phone (optional), what best describes you, budget (optional) and your message. I use it to answer your enquiry. Basis: your consent, and steps taken at your request before a possible contract (GDPR Art. 6.1.a and 6.1.b).

3. How long I keep it

Contact enquiries: for as long as needed to answer you and to deal with any follow-up, and afterwards only blocked for the legal limitation periods. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete your data.

I do not sell your data. It is processed on my behalf by these service providers, who may process it outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards required by law (such as standard contractual clauses):

  • Calendly, which handles call bookings.
  • Google, which supplies the typeface used on this website and runs the Google reviews page the site links to.

I may also share data when the law requires it.

5. Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, or to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the use made of it before. Write to info@yourpropertyvalencia.com. If you think your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), www.aepd.es.
