About

I work with you as a strategic partner, not a salesperson

I’m Sara Muñoz, and before anything else, I’m an investor. I built my own portfolio in Spain, so I know what it takes to buy well: the numbers, the risks, the paperwork and the patience.

Today I put that experience to work for you, as an independent intermediary helping investors find the right opportunities in Spain.

My story

I grew up near Barcelona and have called Valencia home for years. I got into real estate for one reason: freedom. By 30, three properties had given me the security to choose my own path.

After years at a large agency, I wanted to work differently: more personal, more honest, more strategic. So I went independent.

As an investor myself, I look past the pretty photos for hidden potential, always with the long term in mind:

  • Which strategy fits your goals?
  • Does the location support that strategy?
  • Is it future-proof?
  • What about returns, regulation and growth potential?

I don’t sell houses. I protect your investment. You get a complete, worry-free service with your interests first, from the first viewing to the notary.

My goal? That you invest with confidence, clarity and peace of mind. Sara Muñoz

What I believe in

My values

Transparency

Honest advice. Clear communication. No surprises.

You should have the full picture before you decide. I share the numbers, costs, risks and opportunities openly, and I’ll tell you what I genuinely think, even when the answer is not to buy.

Independent Advice

I don’t sell you a house. I’m on your side.

I don’t sell properties, I advise you. My role is to understand your goals, challenge your assumptions when needed and help you make decisions that are right for you, never decisions that simply benefit me.

Strategy First

The right investment starts with the right strategy.

We don’t start with a list of properties and try to make one fit. We start with your goals, capital, risk and expectations, define the right strategy, and then look for the opportunity.

Purpose
