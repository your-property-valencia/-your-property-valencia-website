About

My name is Sara Muñoz. I'm originally from a town near Barcelona and have lived in sunny Valencia for several years. Today I work as an independent intermediary for investors looking for the right opportunities in Spain.

My story

I'm originally from a town near Barcelona and I've lived in sunny Valencia for several years now. I got into real estate because I wanted to learn and build my own portfolio, to have a bit more freedom — and at 30, with just three properties, I had the financial security to choose the path I wanted to follow.

After many years at a large real estate agency, I felt increasingly drawn to working independently — guiding investors in a more personal, honest and strategic way, without the pressure of a sales floor.

Today I work as an independent intermediary for investors looking for the right opportunities in Spain. As an investor myself, I look beyond just a beautiful property — I look for the hidden potential of distressed assets. My focus is always the long term: what strategy is best for your long-term goals? Does the location fit your strategy? Is it future-proof? What about return, regulation and growth potential?

Most important, I don't sell houses — I provide a comprehensive, worry-free service, always protecting your interest. From viewings and negotiations to contracts, municipal registrations, legal coordination and the final transfer, I take care of the whole process. Transparent, dedicated and personal.

My goal? That you invest with confidence, clarity and peace of mind. If you're ready to take the next step and turn your investment plans into reality, I'd be glad to guide you. — Sara Muñoz

What I believe in

My values

Transparency

Honest advice. Clear communication. No surprises.

I believe you should have the full picture before making an investment decision. I share the numbers, costs, risks and opportunities openly, and I'll always tell you what I genuinely think — even when the answer is not to buy.

Independent Advice

I'm on your side.

I don't sell properties. I advise you. My role is to understand your goals, challenge assumptions when necessary and help you make decisions that are right for you — not decisions based on what benefits me.

Strategy First

The right investment starts with the right strategy.

I don't believe in starting with a list of properties and trying to make one fit. We start with your goals, capital, risk and expectations, define the strategy that makes sense for you, and then look for the right opportunity.

Purpose

My mission

To help people invest in Valencia with clarity, confidence and a strategy that is truly their own.

I started investing in property myself because I wanted greater financial freedom and more control over my life. Today, I use that experience, together with my local knowledge and network in Valencia, to help other investors make informed decisions and navigate the Spanish property market without having to figure everything out alone.

I want my clients to have someone they can genuinely trust on the ground — someone who knows their strategy, understands their goals and will always give them an honest opinion.

Because investing in another country shouldn't mean investing blindly.
