What goes wrong

Common mistakes beginners make

Most first-time investors lose money in the same predictable ways. Each of these can quietly wipe out your return.

No clear strategy

Buying a property before deciding what it's actually for.

Overpaying

Without local knowledge, it's easy to pay above real market value.

Wrong neighbourhood

A great street for living in isn't always a great one for renting.

Underestimating renovation

Costs and timelines that turn a good deal into a bad one.

Miscalculating returns

Optimistic rent, forgotten expenses, and the numbers stop working.

Ignoring legal & tax

Spanish regulation and taxation have real consequences if missed.

Good to know

FAQ for beginners

How much budget do I need to start?

Plan for at least 20% of the purchase price plus expenses (tax, notary, registration and agency costs). In practice, that means starting from around €50,000 for a first investment. We map your realistic range on the discovery call before looking at anything.

Can I get financing as a non-resident?

Yes, typically up to 60–70% of the purchase price. Sometimes that local financing can be complemented with a personal loan in your home country. We always analyse what's the best strategy and capital structure for your situation.

What are the legal basics of buying in Spain?

You'll need an NIE number, a Spanish bank account, and a notary to formalise the purchase. Expect purchase costs on top of the price for tax, notary and registration. I coordinate all of this so nothing is missed.

Do I need to be in Valencia for the purchase?

Not necessarily. Almost everything can be done remotely. If you can't travel for the signing at the notary, you can grant a power of attorney so someone you trust can sign on your behalf.
