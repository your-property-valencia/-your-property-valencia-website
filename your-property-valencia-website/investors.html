The opportunity

Where the returns are, for those who already know how to invest

Valencia isn't undiscovered any more — but it's still inefficient. The gap between prime and emerging districts, and between listed and off-market pricing, is where experienced investors are finding their edge.

30–40%

Lower price per m² than comparable areas of Madrid or Barcelona

4–5%

Typical net yield on turnkey properties in prime, fully-let districts

7%+

Net yield achievable in emerging districts with the right repositioning

  • Short-term rental licensing restrictions are pushing demand — and yield — toward long-term and room-rental strategies
  • Limited prime supply is compressing yields in the centre, widening the gap with emerging districts
  • International and institutional buyers are increasingly active, tightening the window for off-market access

Figures are indicative, based on recent market activity and client projects — not a guarantee of future results. Ask for current data on your target districts on a strategy call.
